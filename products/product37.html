HaremPrints — Men Plus Size Size Guide

The HaremPrints are designed with a generous loose fit through the hip and thigh. The waistband is elastic or drawstring for full adjustability. If between two sizes, choose your usual size — the loose cut accommodates. For extra room, size up.

How to Measure

  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, relaxed.
  • Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.
  • Inseam: Measure from crotch to mid-ankle (cuffed harem length).
  • When between sizes, your usual size is fine — the harem cut is forgiving.
