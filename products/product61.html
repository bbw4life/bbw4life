How often should I use the DeepRepair Mask?

Healthy hair: once weekly is ideal. Gentle enough for frequent use — queens with fine hair can leave on 15 minutes for a lighter treatment.

How to Use It & Why It Works
Apply after shampooing Towel dry until damp, then apply generously mid-length to tips (root to tip for severe damage). Comb through evenly.
Leave on, rinse cool 15–45 minutes under a shower cap for maximum repair, then rinse with cool water to seal the cuticle and lock in benefits.
Penetrates, rebuilds, shines Restores moisture from inside the shaft and rebuilds broken protein bonds — visible shine and frizz-free hair from the first use.
Can I use this on chemically relaxed or permed hair?
›

Yes — particularly beneficial for chemically processed hair. Relaxers and perms weaken protein structure and strip moisture. DeepRepair rebuilds that structure and restores hydration. Queens with relaxed hair report dramatically less breakage and improved manageability.

How quickly will I see results?
›

Most queens notice softer, smoother hair from the first use. Frizz reduction is typically visible from application one. For breakage repair, 2–4 weeks of weekly use produces cumulative results. Severe damage: transformative results within 3–6 weeks twice-weekly.

Can I use it as a leave-in treatment?
›

No — DeepRepair is a rinse-out treatment. Leaving it in causes build-up that weighs hair down. Apply to damp hair, leave 15–45 minutes, rinse thoroughly with cool water. The benefits are locked into the strand after rinsing.

Is this suitable for natural, afro-textured hair?
›

Absolutely — particularly effective for natural and coily hair which needs deep penetrating hydration. Restores suppleness, defines curl pattern and reduces frizz significantly. Queens with natural hair report some of the most dramatic results.
