LooseJeans — Men Plus Size Size Guide

The LooseJeans are cut with a relaxed fit through seat, hip and thigh. If between sizes, choose your usual size. For extra room in the seat and thigh, size up one.

How to Measure

  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, relaxed.
  • Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your seat.
  • Inseam: Measure from your crotch to your ankle.
  • Between sizes? Your usual size works — the relaxed cut gives natural room.
