How often should I use the LED Facial Mask?
›

3 to 5 times a week, 10–20 minutes per session, gives the most consistent results. Cleansed, dry skin before every session helps the light absorb properly.

When will I start seeing results?
›

Most queens notice a brighter, smoother glow within 2 weeks of consistent use, with fuller results building over 4–6 weeks. Consistency matters more than intensity.

Is this safe for sensitive skin?
›

Yes — LED light therapy is gentle and non-invasive, suitable for most skin types. Always keep eyes closed during your session and start with shorter sessions if your skin is very sensitive.
