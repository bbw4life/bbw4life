BeltedBanquet Dress — Size Guide

Designed with a high-waist silhouette and a cinching belt that defines your shape in one motion. Measure at the fullest part of your bust, waist, and hips. When between sizes, go up for the most comfortable drape — the belt shapes the rest.

How to Measure Yourself

  • Step 1: Measure around the fullest part of your bust.
  • Step 2: Measure around your natural waistline.
  • Step 3: Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
  • When between sizes, choose the larger size — the belt cinches in for a defined waist.
