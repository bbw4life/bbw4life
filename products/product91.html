"Mini" doesn't mean impractical — it means you finally stop carrying things you don't need.

Is it really big enough for daily use?
›

Yes — for the essentials, absolutely. Phone, wallet, keys, and a couple small extras fit comfortably. It's designed for queens who are done carrying a bag heavier than they need.

What does "Japanese-style" mean here?
›

Clean lines, no unnecessary hardware, and a texture that reads understated rather than flashy — minimalist design philosophy applied to an everyday mini bag.

Which color is easiest to style?
›

Black is the most versatile for everyday wear. Want something that stands out a little more? Green is a fresh, unexpected pop against neutral outfits.

Can I wear it crossbody or just handheld?
›

The adjustable strap works both ways — shorten it for a handheld/shoulder look, or lengthen it for hands-free crossbody wear.

How do I care for it?
›

Clean: Wipe with a soft, slightly damp cloth to remove daily marks.

Store: Keep out of direct sunlight when not in use to preserve the texture.
