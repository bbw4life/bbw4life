VintageSquare Dress — Size Guide

The VintageSquare Dress is designed with a relaxed but flattering fit that celebrates curves. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a comfortable, flowing silhouette. The printed fabric has natural stretch and movement.

How to Measure Your Body

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
  • Waist: Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waist.
  • Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.
  • When between sizes, always choose the larger size for maximum comfort and confidence.

Is the square neckline flattering on plus size women?
›

The square neckline is one of the most universally flattering necklines for plus size women. It creates a clean horizontal line that broadens the shoulders, opens the chest with elegance, and frames the face beautifully. Style experts call it the "built-in confidence neckline" — you will understand why the first time you put this dress on.

Can I wear this dress to formal occasions?
›

Absolutely. With the right accessories, it is completely appropriate for weddings, office functions, church, graduations, and formal dinners. Add gold or pearl jewelry and block heels for formal settings. Pair with sneakers and a denim jacket for casual outings. This dress does it all.

Which color should I choose — Black or Red?
›

Both are stunning — queens who order both never regret it. Black is your everyday powerhouse — versatile, authoritative, endlessly stylish. Red is your statement piece — passion and pure confidence in fabric form. Black for professional events, Red for celebrations. And if you're bundling, the savings make getting both completely worth it.

How does the sizing run?
›

The VintageSquare Dress runs true to size with a slightly relaxed fit designed for comfort and movement. Sizes XL to 4XL. If between sizes, size up — the extra room creates a beautiful, flowing silhouette. Check the size chart above for your measurements.

What shoes pair best with this dress?
›

Block or kitten heels for formal events. Strappy sandals for evenings, especially in Red. White sneakers for casual daytime. Mary Janes or ballet flats for the vintage Black look. Wedge sandals for comfort at long events. Every shoe works — this dress plays well with everything.

How do I care for this dress?
›

Washing: Hand wash cold with gentle detergent, or delicate cycle in a mesh bag. Avoid hot water to protect the print colors. Drying: Lay flat or hang gently, away from direct sunlight. Ironing: Low heat, inside out with a pressing cloth. Queens who care for their VintageSquare properly report wearing it 30+ times with no fading.
