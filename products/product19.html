Measure your waist and hips. When between sizes, choose the larger size — lace should drape beautifully, never pull.

How to Measure

  • Waist: At your natural waistline, narrowest point.
  • Hips: Around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Tip: Between sizes? Always go up.
