ColorStilettos — Size Guide

EU sizing. Measure heel to longest toe. Between sizes? Go up — especially important with a stiletto heel.

How to Measure Your Foot

  • Step 1: Place your foot flat on a piece of paper.
  • Step 2: Mark the tip of your longest toe and the back of your heel.
  • Step 3: Measure in cm. When between sizes, choose the larger size.
