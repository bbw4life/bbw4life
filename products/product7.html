Measure bust, waist and hips. Between sizes? Go up for a relaxed, flattering fit.

How to Measure

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline.
  • Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
  • When between sizes, always choose the larger size.

›

Is a mock neck dress comfortable to wear all evening?
›

Absolutely. The mock neck is soft, not restrictive — it sits gently without feeling tight. Long sleeves add warmth ideal for air-conditioned venues and outdoor evenings without sacrificing comfort.

What shoes and accessories pair best?
›

Keep it simple and let the dress lead. A bold stiletto heel (black or metallic) is perfect. Minimal gold earrings or a delicate bracelet — nothing that competes with the print. A small clutch in black or gold completes the look.

Can I wear this to work or formal events?
›

Yes. The mock neck and long-sleeve design gives it an inherently professional elegance — office, formal meetings, business dinners. Pointed flats for work, heels for the evening. One dress, many worlds.
