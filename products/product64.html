Can I use this every day or will it irritate my skin?
›

Use 2 to 3 times per week — not daily. Very sensitive skin: start once a week and increase gradually. The formula is gentle at the recommended frequency.

Can I use it on my body too or only on my face?
›

Formulated for the face, but many women also use it on neck, décolletage and upper back. Apply the same way: massage gently, leave 2–3 minutes, rinse.

Should I apply anything after rinsing the gel?
›

Yes — apply a moisturizer or serum immediately after. Clean pores absorb more effectively. This is the moment your skincare works best.
