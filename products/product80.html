FlareFit Dress — Size Guide

Designed with a hip-hugging fit that skims, not squeezes. Measure at the fullest part of your bust, waist, and hips. When between sizes, go up for a relaxed, flattering drape.

How to Measure Yourself

  • Step 1: Measure around the fullest part of your bust.
  • Step 2: Measure around your natural waistline.
  • Step 3: Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
  • When between sizes, choose the larger size for the most flattering drape.
